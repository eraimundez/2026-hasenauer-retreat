Capturing and explaining individual variability

Variability Modelling
BSV (ω²) — between-subject: stable patient-level differences (genetics, body composition, disease). Primary target for covariate modelling.

BOV (κ²) — between-occasion: within-patient fluctuations across periods (food, compliance, circadian).

RUV (σ²) — residual: assay error, model misspecification.
Covariate modelling
Covariates enter multiplicatively on individual parameters:
CLi = CLpop · (WTi/WTmedian)θWT · (1 + θSEX · SEXi) · exp(ηi)
Informative covariates reduce unexplained BSV — enabling dose individualisation.
